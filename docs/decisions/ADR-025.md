# ADR-025: Headings weigh more than text, and a search for a page's exact title finds that page first

Status: accepted
Date: 2026-09-13
Amends: ADR-021 (the ranking rules every backend shares)
Relates to: ADR-005, ADR-022

## Context

Checking the migration to Azure, the agent searched every page's title and found that a page came first for its own title only 22 times out of 97. Searching "BPC-157" returned three pages whose "Related" sections link to BPC-157 before the BPC-157 page.

Two causes:

1. **The heading path was stored but not indexed.** A page's title and headings only counted where the body text repeated them, and BM25's length normalisation favours a two-line "Related" section full of the name over a long page about it.
2. **Hybrid search fuses by chunk.** A page's title-matching chunk carries its keyword rank, while the meaning ranking, which keeps only matches that stand out, may keep another page's chunk and not this one. A page in both lists beats a page in one, however well the one ranked.

## Decision

1. **The heading path is an indexed FTS5 column,** `heading`, holding the page title and the headings above the chunk, and BM25 weighs it 5 times the body text. Existing indexes are dropped and rebuilt on start, as for a tokenizer change (ADR-021).
2. **A search whose words are exactly a page's title puts that page first,** in keyword and hybrid mode, ignoring case, accents and punctuation (core `sameWords`). The rest of the ranking is unchanged. It is a rule every backend applies, like the match rules of ADR-021, and the conformance suite tests it.
3. **Fusion stays per chunk** (ADR-022). Fusing per page was tried: it moved hybrid title lookups from 67 to 69 of 96, too little to change every hybrid search for.

## Consequences

1. Measured on the peptide wiki, 96 pages. A page first for its own title: 96 of 96 in both modes, against 22 before. Without the exact-title rule, the heading weight alone gives 92 of 96 in keyword mode and 67 in hybrid.
2. `pnpm eval` is unchanged: recall@5 0.83 keyword and 1.00 hybrid, and the 9 questions with no answer still return nothing in both.
3. The weight 5 was chosen, not tuned against a larger set; the eval's 30 real queries (ROADMAP) will test it.
4. A query that is a title plus other words ("BPC-157 dosage") gets no special treatment: BM25 and the heading weight decide.
